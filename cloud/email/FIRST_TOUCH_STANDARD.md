# Tanzer Anderson First-Touch Standard

Canonical operating rule for every new outbound prospect email.

## Core architecture

Every first touch must follow this sequence:

1. **Specific observation** — open with a verified fact about the recipient, firm, active role, practice, hiring pattern, or operating context. No generic compliments.
2. **Reject the generic pitch** — explicitly frame the note as something narrower than a broad agency or vendor introduction.
3. **One concrete test** — propose one role, search, or use case as the smallest useful way to judge Tanzer Anderson.
4. **Proof mechanism** — show exactly how the search would be attacked: target pools, screening logic, ranking/evidence, and handoff structure.
5. **Commercial boundary** — make clear what is and is not being provided before purchase or engagement.
6. **Low-friction next step** — one simple decision. No forced meeting requirement.

Tone: concise, courteous, precise, senior, non-generic. Default length: 100–160 words before signature.

## Search-firm / agency partner first touch

Use the Norton Rose Fulbright proof-of-concept structure adapted for white-label or overflow work:

- Name one difficult assignment that fits the partner's practice.
- Show the exact market-mapping approach in one compact proof block.
- State that the partner keeps the client relationship, commercial terms, candidate contact, assessment, and final presentation.
- Invite one contained test search rather than a broad partnership discussion.

Default close: `If the work is useful, we can discuss whether Tanzer Anderson belongs in your overflow or partner rotation.`

## Direct-employer first touch

- Anchor to one live or credibly inferred hiring need.
- Show the target-market map and screening dimensions that would be used.
- Do not claim candidate interest, availability, or contact that has not been verified.
- Ask for permission to demonstrate the search approach on that one role or to enter the employer's approved agency process.

Default close: `If the approach is useful, we can discuss whether Tanzer Anderson belongs in your approved search-firm mix.`

## Commercial Talent Sprint first touch

The Sprint does **not** give away the completed work before purchase. The proof before purchase is relevance and transparency, not free delivery.

Required sequence:

- Explain why the prospect's operating model makes one difficult search a good Sprint use case.
- Say: `Rather than send a general overflow-research pitch, I’d suggest a simple test: put one difficult search through the 72-hour Commercial Talent Sprint and judge us on the handoff.`
- Name the exact type of search recommended for that prospect.
- Explain that the scope is fully visible before purchase, while the research, ranking, evidence notes, source links, outreach copy, and private handoff room are produced only after purchase and accepted intake.
- State the commercial boundary: `$3,000 fixed. No placement fee. Your team retains every candidate and client interaction.`
- No call requirement.

Default close: `If that is useful, review the scope and purchase the Sprint. We can handle the rest in writing.`

## Non-negotiable first-touch rules

- Greeting required.
- One recipient wherever practical; no mass-looking first touch.
- Recipient/company/role specificity required.
- No generic `I hope you are well` opening.
- No abrupt `Take your X role for example` opening.
- No unsupported claims.
- No wall of text.
- Preserve Tanzer Anderson Private Monograph / Canvas visual treatment.
- Sender identity: Henry Anderson, Managing Director - Strategy and Business Development, director@tanzeranderson.com.
- External send remains locked until separately authorized.

## Migration rule

Any existing unsent draft that is genuinely a first-touch outbound message should be rewritten to this standard while preserving its recipient, verified facts, role-specific evidence, compliance route, visual shell, and send lock. Internal controls, QA drafts, follow-ups, replies, templates, meeting workflows, procurement actions, and already-sent messages are excluded.
