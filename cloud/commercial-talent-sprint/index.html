Scope clarity

Questions buyers usually ask.

The answers below are part of the fixed commercial specification and should be read before purchase.

When does the 72-hour clock begin?

After payment, complete intake, and written acceptance of the role as in scope. Tanzer Anderson identifies material missing information before acceptance rather than starting an incomplete search.

Are the candidates already interested?

No. The standard Sprint is a research and talent-intelligence product. Prospects are not represented as interested, available, or open to a move unless that has been separately verified and stated.

Who contacts candidates?

The purchasing company retains candidate contact, evaluation, interview, client, and hiring decisions. A separate written agreement is required for Tanzer Anderson to conduct outreach or recruiting activity.

Can the Sprint cover more than one role?

The $3,000 product covers one accepted role. Related roles, multiple locations, unusually broad geographies, or additional candidate volume may require a separate scope.

What happens after delivery?

One written refinement is included. Additional research, candidate validation, outreach execution, or ongoing delivery capacity can be scoped separately.
