1-5: Better inference

1. Historical event-reaction library

Measure NQ, yields, VIX, semiconductors, and mega-cap reactions after each CPI, PCE, payrolls, GDP, FOMC, and Treasury auction surprise. The morning brief would state the closest historical analogs and typical reaction paths.

2. Options and gamma pressure map

Use delayed public CBOE option-chain data to estimate zero-gamma, major open-interest walls, skew stress, and expiration concentration without buying an options API.

3. Index concentration attribution

Translate premarket moves in the largest Nasdaq-100 constituents into approximate index-point contribution so the report distinguishes broad risk appetite from one-stock index distortion.

4. Cross-asset lead-lag engine

Track whether 2Y/10Y yields, DXY, VIX/VXN, semiconductors, credit, and energy are leading or merely confirming NQ, then score the stability of the relationship by regime.

5. Treasury liquidity and buyback pulse

Add auction tails, bid-to-cover, indirect-dealer participation, settlement pressure, Treasury buybacks, and refunding changes to identify liquidity events that can affect duration-sensitive technology.

6-10: Better operations

6. Source-reliability memory and contradiction graph

Keep a rolling record of which outlets broke accurate market-moving stories, which repeated stale narratives, and where high-authority sources disagree.

7. Scenario calibration scorecard

At the next close, automatically grade each scenario, trigger, invalidation, and dominant-driver judgment. Publish rolling hit rates and Brier-style calibration instead of relying on anecdotal memory.

8. Pre-event flash estimates

Generate a one-page browser update 20 minutes before tier-five events and immediately after the release, showing the surprise, revisions, initial cross-asset reaction, and whether the morning thesis survived.

9. Personalized risk-budget overlay

Translate event density, volatility, overnight range, and scenario confidence into Henry-specific maximum exposure, stop budget, and no-trade conditions without changing the underlying intelligence estimate.

10. Private-access shield and multi-channel delivery

Add encrypted browser access, optional email attachment, and a phone-ready executive card while preserving the zero-paid-API rule and independence from ChatGPT membership.

Recommended implementation order

First: scenario calibration, event-reaction library, and index contribution. These create a measurable learning loop. Second: options/gamma and Treasury liquidity. Third: flash estimates, personalized risk overlay, and private delivery.

The current release already lays the foundation for items 1, 3, 4, 6, and 7 through daily archives, cross-asset inputs, source health, contradiction flags, and structured scenario records.
