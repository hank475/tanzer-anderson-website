METHODOLOGY AND GOVERNANCE

NQ Intelligence Firm

How the morning estimate is collected, scored, challenged, and disseminated.

Collection doctrine

Official sources receive precedence for FOMC, Federal Reserve, BLS, BEA, Census, Treasury, CFTC, and SEC facts. Public market quotes and public news indexes add context. No paid API key or ChatGPT runtime is used.

Analytical pipeline

Items are normalized, deduplicated, mapped to NQ driver clusters, scored for NQ relevance, source authority, recency, and corroboration, then compared with market internals. Conflicting high-authority signals are surfaced rather than averaged away.

Market framework

The regime score combines NQ direction, semiconductor leadership, mega-cap breadth, volatility, Treasury yields, the dollar, and credit-versus-duration behavior. Missing data lowers confidence and is never replaced with a synthetic value.

Scenario doctrine

Scenario weights are conditional model weights. Each scenario requires a price trigger, cross-asset confirmation, and a stated invalidation. They are not calibrated probabilities, signals, or instructions to trade.

Dissemination

Each weekday edition is rendered as an exact two-page PDF, evidence JSON, browser dashboard, RSS entry, and durable archive record. The source-health table documents failures and latency.

Known limits

Public quotes may be delayed; news indexes can omit reporting; CFTC positioning is weekly; proprietary exchange depth, dealer gamma, and institutional order-flow data are not included.

Source families

Federal Reserve RSS and calendars; BLS release calendar and feeds; BEA release schedule; Census indicators; Treasury yield curve and auctions; CFTC Traders in Financial Futures; SEC EDGAR company submissions; public market charts with daily fallback; GDELT and Google News RSS; Nasdaq public earnings calendar.

Fail-closed policy: when a lane is unavailable, the report states the degradation and withholds unsupported inference.
