# Tanzer Anderson Paper-Wrap Email Standard

This is the canonical visual and delivery standard for all future Tanzer Anderson email correspondence.

## Required presentation

- Use the approved August 4 paper-wrap design: a restrained paper-inspired outer frame, a clear white reading area, a text-based Tanzer Anderson wordmark, and a fine gold rule.
- Use table-based HTML with inline styles for Gmail and Outlook compatibility.
- Every outbound message must greet the recipient by first name when known; otherwise greet the named team.
- Place the primary offer immediately below the greeting.
- Display `director@tanzeranderson.com` in the signature and reply pathway.
- Use the title `Managing Director – Strategy and Business Development`.
- Render `Henry Anderson` in dark cursive text using robust system-font fallbacks.
- Include the Dallas P.O. Box and no-outreach language in external commercial correspondence.
- Include a complete plain-text fallback.
- The core design must remain fully readable with images blocked.
- Do not use a remote image, embedded image, CID image, or pre-rendered artwork as the email background.
- Do not use `Private & Confidential`, `Private Correspondence`, internal-review language, placeholders, or raw internal syntax.
- Preserve all release, approval, candidate-interest, suppression, and no-send controls.

## Canonical visual structure

1. Warm ivory outer field.
2. Restrained paper-texture effect created with HTML/CSS only.
3. White reading area with a fine neutral border.
4. Tanzer Anderson wordmark rendered as live text.
5. Fine gold rule.
6. Personalized greeting.
7. Concise message body with the offer immediately after the greeting.
8. Strong text CTA.
9. Dark cursive signature, exact title, and `director@tanzeranderson.com`.
10. P.O. Box and no-outreach footer.

## Permanently retired design

The image-background design formerly stored as `email-standard/assets/tanzer-background-01.jpg` and its base64 source fragments is rejected and retired. It must not be recreated, regenerated, linked, embedded, referenced, or used in any future Tanzer Anderson email.

All email-generation, draft-creation, QA, scheduling, and outreach workflows must use `email-standard/template.html` unless Henry explicitly approves a different design for a specific message.
