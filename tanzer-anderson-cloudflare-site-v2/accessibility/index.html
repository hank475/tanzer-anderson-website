Accessibility

A premium experience should also be a usable one.

Tanzer Anderson is committed to making the public website understandable, navigable, and compatible with common assistive technologies.

Our approach

The site uses semantic structure, keyboard-accessible navigation, visible focus behavior, readable contrast, responsive layouts, reduced-motion support, and descriptive form labels.

Continuous improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing practice. Changes to content, browsers, devices, or assistive technology may reveal issues that require correction.

Report a barrier

Send the page address, the issue encountered, and the device or assistive technology used to clientservices@tanzeranderson.com. Tanzer Anderson will make a good-faith effort to respond and provide the information through an accessible alternative.
