About Tanzer Anderson

Judgment is the product. Search is how we deliver it.

Tanzer Anderson is a founder-led executive recruiting and talent advisory firm built for consequential hiring, scarce capability, and leadership decisions that cannot be reduced to volume.

The firm

Small by design. Senior by default.

Clients work directly with the people responsible for the judgment, not through layers of account management. That creates sharper calibration, faster decisions, and clearer accountability.

We combine executive-search discipline with practical staffing delivery and a structured workforce-intelligence layer. The result is a search process that explains not only who is available, but what the market is signaling and what the client should do next.

“The person who scopes the search should remain accountable for the result.”

Our standard is simple: anticipate the question, show the evidence, make a recommendation, and make the next action easy.

Operating principles

01

Evidence over theater

Research, validation, and explicit confidence levels replace inflated certainty and presentation for its own sake.

02

Precision over volume

A smaller, decision-ready slate is more valuable than a large pipeline that transfers the evaluation burden back to the client.

03

Care without friction

Communication should answer what happened, why it matters, our recommendation, and what—if anything—the client needs to do.
