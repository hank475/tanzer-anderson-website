Privacy

A straightforward statement about how website information is handled.

Tanzer Anderson collects only the information reasonably needed to respond to an inquiry and operate the public website.

Last updated July 31, 2026.

Information you provide

When you contact Tanzer Anderson, you may provide your name, business contact information, organization, and details about a role or engagement. Do not submit confidential personal, medical, financial, or protected information through the public form.

How information is used

Information is used to respond to inquiries, evaluate potential engagements, maintain business records, protect the website, and improve service. Tanzer Anderson does not sell personal information.

Service providers

The website may use Cloudflare for hosting, security, and traffic delivery and Google Workspace for business communications and document handling. Those providers process information under their own terms and security practices.

Retention and requests

Business inquiry information is retained only as reasonably needed for the purpose collected, legal obligations, and legitimate business records. Questions or requests may be sent to clientservices@tanzeranderson.com.
