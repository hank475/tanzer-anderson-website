Frequently asked questions

Is there a placement fee?

No. RaaS uses a per-opening recruiting fee and does not add a percentage-of-salary placement fee.

When does a search officially start?

After payment is confirmed or a prepaid credit is allocated and the completed official intake and agreed criteria are accepted by Tanzer Anderson. Both conditions are required.

When is a search fulfilled?

When the client selects three distinct Tanzer Anderson candidates for interview or selects one Tanzer Anderson candidate for offer, whichever occurs first.

What does the two-week target mean?

Initial candidate submissions are targeted within 10 business days after the official start. It is not a completion or expiration deadline.

How is Specialized determined?

During intake, before acceptance, based on factors that materially increase sourcing effort.

Are paid postings included?

No. Paid media and other third-party costs require separate approval and client funding.

Can I submit a Director or VP role?

Yes, but it routes to separate Leadership or Executive Search rather than RaaS.
