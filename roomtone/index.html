How it works

A real signal, with hard limits

RoomTone measures the change in a wall echo between two known phone positions using one microphone.

01

Position

Place the phone at the near mark, then the far mark, with its bottom edge aimed at one large bare wall.

02

Echo

The speaker emits short coded chirps. The microphone resolves the direct pulse and the wall reflection at each position.

03

Cancel latency

Subtracting the near echo delay from the far echo delay removes the browser’s unknown input/output latency.

04

Estimate

The known distance difference and measured time difference yield sound speed, then air temperature with humidity compensation.

What RoomTone cannot promise

A bedroom has temperature gradients, and reflections vary with wall material, cases, placement and browser audio processing. This is not a safety, medical, laboratory or HVAC commissioning instrument. Accurate wall marks and stable placement matter more than screen precision.
